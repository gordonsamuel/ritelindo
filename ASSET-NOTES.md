# Catatan aset

`public/assets/store-hero.webp` dibuat menggunakan built-in image generation dan dioptimasi menjadi WebP. Ini ilustrasi konsep, bukan foto produk/proyek asli. Tidak ada logo merek pihak lain atau orang pada gambar. Label ilustrasi ditampilkan di hero.

Prompt final:

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: landscape photographic concept illustration for the right half of a B2B retail shelving website hero, image only
Primary request: a credible modern Indonesian small minimarket interior fitted with empty white metal gondola retail shelving, navy blue upright posts and orange header strips.
Scene/backdrop: compact clean retail store with orderly parallel aisles and polished gray floor, neutral light walls, bright daylight entering front windows.
Subject: professionally installed modular gondola shelving, fully empty shelves, precise consistent shelf geometry and realistic supports.
Style/medium: photorealistic architectural interior photography, polished professional product scene.
Composition/framing: landscape wide angle at human eye level, three-quarter view along orderly aisles, shelves prominently visible, straight verticals, balanced framing.
Lighting/mood: bright neutral daylight, soft natural shadows, clean welcoming practical space.
Materials/textures: white powder-coated metal shelves, navy powder-coated uprights, orange plain header strips, subtly reflective polished gray floor.
Constraints: no people, no brands, no text, no letters, no labels, no signage, no watermark, no UI, no typography, no products on shelves. Generate exactly one image.
```

Tidak ada klaim bahwa warna ilustrasi merupakan warna resmi Ritelindo. Wordmark website memakai teks; favicon adalah motif rak sederhana, bukan logo resmi.
